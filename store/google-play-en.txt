## Full Description (max 4000 characters)
DotRec is a fast, minimalist, and genuinely free screen recording application designed for Android.

Inspired by Nothing OS aesthetics: clean monochrome design, a single bold red accent reserved for recording, the iconic Doto dot-matrix typeface, and elegant rounded controls. Both light and dark themes are fully supported from day one.

No advertisements, no watermarks, no paywalls, and no time limits. DotRec runs 100% offline and does not even declare the INTERNET permission!

● 1-TAP RECORDING START:
  • From the main app screen with tactile, responsive feedback;
  • From the Android Quick Settings shade (tile with live duration counter);
  • From your home screen using the stylish 2×1 widget (light & dark modes);
  • Via the optional floating pill overlay that automatically hides from the final video.

● SMART PRESETS & FULL MANUAL CONTROL:
  • Instant presets tailored for everyday needs: Fast, Balanced, Quality;
  • Custom mode for enthusiasts:
    - Resolution: from lightweight 480p up to crisp 1080p+ (respecting your device display);
    - Frame rate: 30, 60, 90, and 120 FPS (honestly capped by your screen refresh rate);
    - Bitrate: smooth slider from 4 to 24 Mbps;
    - Video codecs: hardware H.264 or highly efficient HEVC (H.265).

● CRYSTAL-CLEAR AUDIO:
  • Microphone audio capture for voiceovers, tutorials, and commentary;
  • Internal system audio recording (AudioPlaybackCapture on Android 10+);
  • Clean simultaneous mixing of microphone and internal app/game sound.

● EFFORTLESS WORKFLOW:
  • Countdown timer before recording (None / 3s / 5s);
  • Pause and resume recording anytime;
  • Interactive notification shade control with chronometer, Pause, and Stop actions;
  • Instant "Recording saved" notification: preview, share, or delete in one tap.

● IN-APP MEDIA LIBRARY:
  • Videos saved cleanly to the standard Movies/DotRec folder;
  • Built-in gallery with thumbnails, durations, and file sizes;
  • Pull-to-refresh, open with any video player, or share with favorite apps.

● UNCOMPROMISED PRIVACY & RELIABILITY:
  • ZERO network permission — INTERNET is completely absent from the Android manifest;
  • 0% analytics, 0% tracking, 0% telemetry;
  • Low storage guard: prevents starting if free space < 200 MB, safely finalizes video before 100 MB threshold;
  • Permissions requested strictly on demand (Microphone only when enabled, Overlay only when toggled).

● ACCESSIBILITY & 7 LANGUAGES:
  • TalkBack optimized with periodic timer announcements every 10 seconds;
  • Responsive UI scaling up to 130%;
  • Available in 7 languages: English, Русский, Español, Deutsch, Français, Português, Українська (system auto-detect or manual selector).

Record gameplay highlights, bug repros, video calls, and tutorials cleanly and without distractions with DotRec!
